Frequently Asked Questions
How do I register my SpinBot product for warranty?
Visit the SpinBot Warranty Portal, select your product model, select purchase date and marketplace (Amazon, Flipkart, SpinBot Store), and upload your invoice. An instant Registration ID will be generated.
How can I track my shipment delivery?
Enter your AWB or Order ID in the quick tracking box above or visit the SpinBot Shiprocket Portal for live courier tracking.
What is the SpinBot Device & Gear Tester?
The Gear Tester is an online utility to test touch actuation, button latency, cooling fan speeds, and stereo sound channels on mobile gaming triggers and accessories. Access it at spinbotgeartest.netlify.app.
What defects are covered under warranty?
SpinBot warranty covers all internal manufacturing flaws and electrical defects for 6 to 12 months. Physical drops, liquid damage, or modification attempt voids coverage.
