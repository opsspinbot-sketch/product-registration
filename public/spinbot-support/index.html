Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries

How do I register my SpinBot product for warranty?
Simply visit our Product Registration Portal, select your product model, enter purchase date and platform (Amazon, Flipkart, SpinBot Store), and upload a copy of your purchase invoice. You will receive an instant Registration ID.
How long does delivery take and how can I track my shipment?
Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours. You can track your real-time shipment status anytime by entering your AWB or Order ID in our Shipment Tracking card above or directly on our Shiprocket Tracking page.
What is the SpinBot Device & Gear Tester?
The SpinBot Gear Tester is a free online tool to diagnose trigger responsiveness, touch latencies, audio channels, and hardware performance before or during mobile gaming sessions. Launch it directly via our Device Tester Portal.
What is covered under SpinBot Manufacturer Warranty?
SpinBot warranty covers all manufacturing defects, internal component failures, and electrical malfunctions for 6 to 12 months (depending on product category). Physical damage, water exposure, or unauthorized tampering are not covered.
